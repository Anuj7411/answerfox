03 · Proof-of-Fix

We re-audit the moment you merge, and post the proof.

The receipt lands where the work happens — as a comment on the PR you just merged. You see the score jump, and so does anyone who reviews the PR later.

04 · Drift Guard

Every deploy is watched. The alert arrives with its fix already open.

Regressions happen — a refactor drops a schema, a redirect eats a page. We catch it on the next deploy and open the fix-PR before you'd have noticed.
